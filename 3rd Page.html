Analyzing: Treaty 6's Implementation In Modern Day

The Collaboration Of A Nation

While the implementations of the Treaties have been scrutinized, and fairly so at that, some progress has been made towards its full implementation, which we are going to be exploring. Starting with that in Ottawa, on March 21, 2025 [1 ] , the federal government and the First Nations in the Prairies reached agreements on critical Agricultural Benefit claims. With this being a 3rd wave of agricultural benefit-specific claims have been reached with First Nations this fiscal year. With Settlements totaling $1.2 billion, with the first wave of settlements totaling $1.5 billion, and the second wave of settlements totaling $1.3 billion.

When looking at the implementation of Treaty 6, we should also look at what makes Treaty 6 different from all other Treaties in Canada, for starters, it was involved in all the benefit claims from the last Fiscal Year, and covers approximately 120,000 square kilometers of land. Treaty 6 is also unique because it was negotiated during a time of significant change for the Indigenous peoples of the Plains, as they sought to protect their traditional lands and resources from encroachment by settlers and the Hudson's Bay Company and contains unique terms such as the Medicine Chest clause.
